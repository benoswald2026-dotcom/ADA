Lock of the day

The single strongest consensus pick kicking off in the next 36 hours, college or NFL. It's behind the uprights: make the field goal to see it. Tap once to set your aim (mind the wind), once more to set your power.

Live

Every NFL game in progress: Hashmark's live win probability (trained on 400,000 plays, 2014–2022; as accurate as nflfastR's on 2023–2025) next to ESPN's and Kalshi's live price. Refreshes every 30 seconds. ESPN's feed runs 10–30 seconds behind TV, and Kalshi traders watch TV, so a big gap is usually ESPN catching up, not an edge.

Best bets

Ranked by expected value, not by how likely a bet is to win. An 80% favorite is a bad bet if the price assumes 85%. Only sides where the model and market are within 10 points qualify.

Consensus picks

What ESPN FPI, Dratings, Sagarin, Dimers, OddsShark, Sonny Moore, TeamRankings, nfelo (NFL) and Hashmark's own model expect, averaged, next to the DraftKings spread and total. Strong = the sources on the same side (all of them, or 80% when there are 5+) and the line off by 3+ points (NFL 2+). Picks are logged before kickoff and graded after, so the record below tells you whether to trust them.

Kalshi scan

Every Kalshi winner, spread and total price next to DraftKings (vig removed; spreads and totals only where Kalshi has DraftKings' exact line): today and tomorrow for college, the next three days for the NFL. No API keys needed. A FLAG needs at least 3 points of edge after Kalshi's fee and a price of 20¢ or more.

Player props

Each player's likely range for passing yards, rushing yards, receptions, and receiving yards. The median is the middle outcome; the fair line is where over and under are even. With sportsbook prop lines (Odds API key), it also shows P(over), the better side, and EV.

Backtest, 2023–2025

Each week is predicted using only games played before it, then scored. Nothing was tuned on the test seasons.

Parlay pricer

Pick 2 or 3 teams from this week. The fair price multiplies the model's win probabilities, which assumes the legs are independent.
